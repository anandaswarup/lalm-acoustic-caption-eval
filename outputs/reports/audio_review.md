## Limitations

- One reviewer rated every caption, so inter-annotator agreement cannot be estimated; more reviewers are left for future work.
- The reviewer had read the references and assigned the E18 categories before listening, so the audio judgements may be anchored on them; captions also carry model style cues (E17).
- Only the phrases E18 marked as unsupported by every reference were verified one by one; the verdict shares are therefore not error rates for all caption content.
- Ratings record what one listener hears in the recording; Clotho has no ground-truth source labels.
- With 50 captions per model, proportions are coarse (one caption is 2 percentage points) and the Wilson intervals wide. Pooled and phrase-level intervals treat the four captions of a recording and the phrases of a caption as independent, so they are too narrow; the summary is descriptive.
