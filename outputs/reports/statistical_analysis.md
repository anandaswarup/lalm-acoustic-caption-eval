## Assumptions and procedures

- The unit of analysis is the recording. Recordings are treated as exchangeable draws from the population Clotho's evaluation split represents; captions of the same recording are never treated as independent (human captions are averaged over the five held-out positions, or resampled together for correlations).
- Every comparison is paired: both systems are scored on the same recordings with the same references, metric implementations and aggregation. Five-reference (E12) and four-reference (E13-E14) scores are never compared with each other, because CIDEr-D, SPIDEr and SPIDEr-FL depend on the reference set.
- The tested statistic is the mean of per-recording scores. For BLEU and METEOR the published corpus value comes from corpus n-gram statistics and is reported separately; their tests and CIs are on the per-item mean (D5).
- The sign-flip test assumes that, under the null hypothesis, the two systems' scores of a recording are exchangeable; it tests the mean difference, not a difference in distribution. Bootstrap CIs are percentile intervals, which can be slightly narrow for skewed metrics such as CIDEr-D.
- Multiple comparisons: Holm-Bonferroni within each family, on every recording only (the families are fixed in configs/metrics.yaml before testing). Sensitivity samples and the second length predictor are uncorrected checks.
- Effect sizes (paired d_z) are reported because with 1,045 recordings very small differences, such as raw BERTScore-F1 gaps of about 0.002, can be significant without being meaningful.
- API models (GPT-Audio-1.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash) are not reproducible at temperature 0 (E05); every test is conditional on the frozen captions and does not include run-to-run variation.
