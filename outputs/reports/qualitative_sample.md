## Limitations

- Blinding is imperfect: style can reveal a model (GPT's JSON-like captions are shown as scored, many Qwen captions begin with 'The audio', and the models differ in typical length; see E05-E09 and E14).
- One reviewer annotates the sample, so inter-annotator agreement cannot be estimated and every label reflects a single judgement; more reviewers are left for future work.
- 50 recordings give coarse category proportions per model (one caption is 2 percentage points); the review is exploratory and not used to estimate dataset-wide rates.
