## Limitations

- Words are whitespace tokens, so punctuation attached to a word is not counted separately and the JSON keys of the 21 malformed GPT captions count as words; the valid-for-all sample excludes those recordings.
- Model captions are scored against five references (E11) and held-out human captions against four (E12), so model and human correlations come from different reference sets.
- Correlations are associations over recordings, not effects of length: length varies with the recording and the content of the caption. Formal tests and corrections for multiple comparisons belong to E15.
- CIs are 95% percentile intervals per comparison, uncorrected.
