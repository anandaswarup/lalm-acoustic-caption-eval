## Limitations

- The E17 and E18 rankings come from the 50-recording qualitative sample and one reviewer; the metric rankings from all 1,045 recordings. Their order-stability shares resample different numbers of recordings.
- Kendall's tau over four models takes only seven values and is not tested; it summarises agreement between rankings, not its significance.
- Concordance uses the 200 sampled captions: pairs within a recording share the recording and the references, but the pairs of one recording are not independent (the CI and test resample whole recordings). E18 ratings are one reviewer's judgements.
- Metric correlations are over captions scored against five references (E11); they describe how the metrics vary together, not which metric is right.
